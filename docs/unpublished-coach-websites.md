# Websites for unpublished coach imports

Research date: October 4, 2026.

A name-based search covered all 105 V.O2 imports currently marked `review`. The 19 initial leads have now been checked individually. The result is **16 well-supported coaching or training-site matches** (12 original plus 4 confirmed in follow-up), **4 named coaching profiles on other organizations' sites**, **8 associated sites that do not establish a current coaching offer**, **3 leads still unverified**, and **5 unavailable old websites without a verified replacement**. For the remaining **69**, no suitable coaching website was verified. These categories total 105.

These are research findings, not publication approvals. No coach records, publication statuses, or production settings were changed. The already-identified Tammy Whyte duplicate and the invalid Tucker Robertson profile were outside this search.

“Well-supported” means the sources connect the named coach to a site describing coaching. It does not independently verify qualifications, availability, ownership of an Anystride account, or permission to sell services on their behalf. A shared coaching business website can be a valid match; a coach does not need a personal domain.

## What this changes about the original review

The old “no independently hosted coaching website matched” reason is not a finding that the person is unsuitable. At least some records can be revisited with better evidence: team biographies, renamed domains, and new business websites.

JavaScript-heavy pages and non-English content also need manual review. Ali G. Running Co. exposed substantially more information in the browser than in a basic fetch. Peppe's and Arsport's sites demonstrate why English-only matching can miss relevant content, although that alone does not prove why each earlier import failed.

For a future update, preserve the source URL, review date, identity evidence, and any unresolved service details separately. Confirm the coach's relationship to a site before changing their link; confirm ownership and approved offers before enabling Anystride sales. None of those updates were applied during this research.
